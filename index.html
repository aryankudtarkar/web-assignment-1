Learn with purpose

Small classes, big questions, and a community that makes space for every kind of ambition.

Founded in 1987, Northstar College is an independent liberal arts college set in the heart of a lively college town. Our students learn by doing: in studios, labs, internships, and the wider community.

We believe a great education should be both challenging and supportive. With personal guidance from faculty and a student-to-faculty ratio of 12:1, you can build confidence while discovering what matters to you.
